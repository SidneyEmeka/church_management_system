CHURCH MANAGEMENT SYSTEM 

What Problems It Solves
A church management system (ChMS) helps religious organizations:
Centralize member information - Replace paper records and spreadsheets
Track attendance and engagement - Understand member participation
Manage events and schedules - Coordinate volunteers and activities
Simplify communication - Send announcements, prayer requests, etc.
Handle donations and finances - Track giving and generate reports
Organize small groups - Manage Bible studies, committees, etc.
Core Features to Implement
1. Authentication & Authorization
User registration/login (members & staff)
Role-based access (Admin, Pastor, Volunteer, Member)
JWT token implementation
2. Member Management
Member profiles with contact info, family relationships
Membership status tracking
Baptism/confirmation dates
3. Attendance Tracking
Service attendance recording
Reports and analytics
4. Event & Calendar Management
Service scheduling
Event creation and registration
Recurring events
5. Donation Management
Donation recording
Online giving integration (simulate with mock payment)
Contribution statements
Fund/campaign tracking
6. Communication Tools
Announcement board
Email
Prayer request management (with privacy controls)
